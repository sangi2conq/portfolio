Detailed case studies

How I approach complex delivery

Each case study follows the same practical pattern: situation, role, contribution, tools, outcome and lessons.

01
Transport Voucher Workflow TransformationVolunteering Gold Coast

Situation

A multi-step transport-voucher process was slow, difficult to track and affected by data and hand-off inconsistencies.

My role

Business analysis, process mapping, requirements clarification, validation design, UAT coordination and operational transition.

Contribution

Mapped current and future states, clarified business rules, identified unnecessary steps and strengthened data checks and ownership points.

Outcome

Reduced the processing cycle from approximately three days to one and created a clearer, more repeatable operating process.
